• TCP provides reliable transport along with congestion
control: senders increase their window additively until
they experience loss, and then back off multiplicatively.
Senders also use slow -start and fast-retransmit/fast -
recovery to quickly increase the window and recover from
loss.
• TCP has been a massive success, but senders don’t
react to congestion until queues are already full .  Is
there a better way?
