• Tor provides anonymity for users, preventing attackers
from linking a sender to its receiver.
• There are still ways to attack Tor, namely by correlating
traffic from various points in the network.
• Both Tor and Bitcoin (last lecture) deal, at least
somewhat, with anonymity . But more importantly, they
solve interesting technical problems and use
cryptography (and other techniques) in clever ways.
Understanding how they work and why they’re used will
give you a better sense of how secure you are online.
