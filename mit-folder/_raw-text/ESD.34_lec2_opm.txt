A Tutorial: Object-Process Methodology 
(OPM)
z OPM is a system development methodology that 
integrates many system attributes in one model
z In particular, explicitly represents objects, processes 
and their links
z Gives us a framework for rigorous qualitative system 
thinking, and perhaps quantitative modeling and 
analysis
z Developed by Dr. Dov Dori, modified for System 
Architecture
z A way to think, not the way to think

Objects

stable, unconditional existence for some positive 
duration of time
z Can be physical: visible or tangible and stable in form
z Can be informational: anything that can be apprehended 
intellectually
z Objects have states (which can be changed by 
processes)
z Objects are linked to nouns

Processes
z Defined: A process is the pattern of transformation 
applied to one or more objects
z Cannot hold or touch a process - it is  fleeting
z Generally creation, change, or destruction
z A process relies on at least one object in the pre-
process set
z A process transforms at least one object in the pre-
process set
z A process takes place along a time line
z A process is associated with a verb

33Massachusetts Institute of Technology  © Ed Crawley 2007
Emergence
z A process can be zoomed into sub-processes
z A process emerges from sub-processes
z The process and sub-processes are not linked in any 
explicit manner, as the object decomposes into parts
z Emergence is a powerful feature of systems - parts and 
sub-processes can come together to cause a process to 
emerge
z Emergence sometimes yields the anticipated processes, 
sometimes does not yield the anticipated process and 
sometimes unanticipated processes

34Massachusetts Institute of Technology  © Ed Crawley 2007
Some OPM Process Notes
z When a process is zoomed, affect, instrument and agent 
links move from the boundary of the process to the 
boundary of the sub-process they are actually 
connected to (it could be more than one sub-process)
z If one sub-process is followed by a second without any 
explicit object between, the invocation link is used (only 
from process to process)
z In reverse engineering, objects may “dangle”. This 
indicates all functions have not yet been identified.
z Sometimes it is more convenient in an OPM to use the 
decomposition symbol rather than the zooming “Venn 
diagram” but if you do so, remember that this is just a 
convenience - processes don’t decompose in any linear 
manner

Summary - OPM
z OPM is a means (not the means) of representing 
systems. It is conceptually able to represent a wide 
range of system nature and complexity 
z OPM represents in one graphical model the objects, the 
processes and their interrelationships
z Objects sum to form, and have interrelationships -
structure
z Processes, together with operand objects, yield 
function, and have interrelationships - emergence 
z Objects are related to processes through a small 
number of types of links
