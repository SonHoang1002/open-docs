• Adversarial attacks are different from “normal” failures.
They’re targeted, rarely random, and rarely independent.
Just one successful attack can bring down a system.
• Securing a system starts by specifying our goals ( policy)
and assumptions ( threat model).
• The guard model provides complete mediation . Even
though things can still go wrong, systems that use this
model avoid common pitfalls.
