• P2P Networks are, in theory, inﬁnitely scalable.  They can
improve performance for some applications, and provide
a way to overcome certain aspects of the Internet’s
architecture.  Incentivizing peers to behave is an
important problem.
• CDNs don’t scale in the same way that P2P networks do,
but are more appropriate for some applications, and
provide some features that a P2P network can’t (more on
that in Thursday’s recitation).
