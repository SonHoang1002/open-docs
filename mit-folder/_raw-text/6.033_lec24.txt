• Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency.  Being
decentralized means that there is no bank; in Bitcoin,
everyone is the bank.
• Bitcoin provides a distributed public log called the
blockchain that can be used for purposes other than
digital currency.  It uses proofs-of-work to prevent Sybil
Attacks, since strong identities won’t work.
• In theory, users of Bitcoin are anonymous ; in practice,
it’s not clear how true that is.
